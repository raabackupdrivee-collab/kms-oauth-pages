Privacy Policy

The KMS Backup Integration uses authorized Google Drive access to upload and manage encrypted system backup files.

Data Access

Google Drive access is used for backup storage operations. The permissions granted depend on the application's configured Google OAuth scopes.

Data Storage and Security

Backup archives are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive. Access to backup credentials and encryption keys is restricted.

Data Sharing

Google Drive data is not sold or used for advertising. Backup data is processed for authorized backup operations.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked from the Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application administrator using the support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
